# Tester guide — help Recall remember you (about 45 minutes over 2 days)

Thank you for testing! Recall is an AI interview coach that keeps short notes about your practice so the next session can pick up where you left off. The hackathon needs real people using it over a few days — your honest use is the evidence.

## Before you start: what gets stored

- When you finish setup you'll be asked to agree to store coaching notes. They are **encrypted** and saved on **Walrus**, a public decentralized storage network. Only this coach can decrypt them.
- Notes are short summaries like “Skipped the Result in a STAR answer about a missed deadline.” **Transcripts are for you; memories are for the coach**: your conversations are saved **encrypted** so you can reread them in **History**, but the coach never reads an old session's transcript — only the notes. You can turn history off or delete it in **Settings** (deleting history doesn't delete notes).
- There's **no delete button for notes in Recall yet** (Walrus Memory itself supports permanent deletion by the account owner). Don't share passwords, contact details or information about other people.
- The organizers will only see pseudonymized counts (e.g. “user-2: 12 memories”). Nothing you write is published without your written OK.

## Day 1 — session 1 (≈15 min)

1. Open the site, click **Start practicing** and **Continue with Google**.
2. Complete setup: your real target role (or one you'd like), level, interview date if you have one, 1–3 focus areas, how you like to learn, and the consent checkbox.
3. Choose **Mock interview**. Answer 2–3 questions for real — imperfect answers are useful!
4. Click **End session** and watch “Saving to Walrus”. Saves can take a minute or two.
5. Click **View in History** and open the session: you'll see your transcript next to “What your coach kept from this session”.

## Day 1 — later (≈10 min)

6. Open the coach again → **Drill a weak spot**. (If you refresh the page mid-session, the conversation should reopen where you were.) Practise until you get one scorecard you're happy with. End the session.

## Day 2 or later — the “come back” session (≈15 min), ideally on another device

7. Sign in again and start **Mock interview**. If you're on a different device from Day 1, please tell us which (e.g. “laptop → phone”). Your **first message** should be exactly: *“Let's practice. What should I work on?”*
8. Look at the reply: does it reference your earlier sessions? Expand **Recalled N memories** to see which notes it used.
9. Open **What I remember** (top of the chat) and check the notes look right.

## Optional: the before/after comparison

After at least two earlier sessions, on the home screen switch on **Start without memory — for comparison**, start a mock interview and send the same first message: *“Let's practice. What should I work on?”*. Then start a normal session and send it again. The difference is the whole point of the project.

## Sharing a transcript (only if you want to)

In any chat, the download icon (**Export conversation**) saves a Markdown file to your computer — nothing is sent anywhere. If you're happy for it to be quoted in the project write-up, send the file to the organizer and say so in writing. You can remove names or anything personal first.
